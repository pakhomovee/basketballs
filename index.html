● Basketballs Project

Automatic Basketball Match Analysis

A computer-vision pipeline that turns a single broadcast-style video stream into structured game data — player detection & tracking, team assignment, pose skeletons, ball tracking, jersey-number recognition, and high-level events such as passes and shots. This is a read-only demo viewer: pick a clip and toggle the annotation layers on top of the video.

Pipeline stages
Court Detection Player & Ball Detection Appearance Embedding Tracking & Re-ID Trajectory Smoothing Team Classification Pose Estimation Jersey Numbers Passes & Shots
